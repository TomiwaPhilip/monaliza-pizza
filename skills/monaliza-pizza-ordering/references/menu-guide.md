# Menu guide (for recommendations)

Always take live prices and availability from `get_menu`. This file only helps you recommend.

## Sizes
- Small, 8 inch: 1 person
- Medium, 10 inch: 2 people
- Large, 12 inch: 3-4 people
- Family of 4-5: 1 large + 1 medium, or 2 large if they're hungry. Add wings or garlic bread for kids.

## Pizzas
- **Suya Special** (`suya-special`): the house favourite and best seller. Spicy beef suya with yaji, onions and peppers. Hot! Warn anyone who can't take pepper.
- **Pepperoni** (`pepperoni`): beef pepperoni. Safe choice for kids.
- **BBQ Chicken** (`bbq-chicken`): sweet and smoky, a crowd pleaser.
- **Meat Lovers** (`meat-lovers`): pepperoni, beef sausage, minced beef and chicken. The most filling.
- **Margherita** (`margherita`): vegetarian, mild. Good for kids.
- **Veggie Supreme** (`veggie-supreme`): vegetarian. Peppers, sweetcorn, mushrooms, olives, onions.

## Sides and drinks
- Chicken Wings (6 pcs), peri-peri or BBQ (put the choice in the order notes)
- Garlic Bread (4 slices), Fries
- Coca-Cola, Fanta, Chapman (house-made, large), Bottled Water

## Suggesting extras
- Offer one drink or side once, after the pizzas are chosen ("Would you like some Chapman or chilled drinks with that?"). Don't push if they say no.
- On hot afternoons, suggest Chapman or chilled water.
