# Monaliza Pizza policies

## Ordering
- Pickup only from the collection window on the right side of the kiosk (green "Ready orders" sign). No delivery inside the park, because the owner runs the kiosk alone.
- Opening hours: daily 11:00-22:00. Last WhatsApp order 21:30.
- Maximum 12 pizzas per WhatsApp order. Larger group or party orders must be arranged in person at the kiosk.
- The kitchen starts an order only after Paystack confirms payment.
- Customers must show their order code (e.g. MP-4821) at the window to collect. Staff hand the order only to someone with the code.
- Unclaimed orders are kept warm for 30 minutes after they are marked ready. After that we can't guarantee quality, but the customer can still collect.

## Payment
- WhatsApp orders are paid online only, via the Paystack link (card, bank transfer or USSD). No cash for WhatsApp orders. Walk-up customers at the kiosk can still pay cash.
- A payment link is valid for 30 minutes. After that the order expires and the customer can ask for a new link.
- If a payment fails, nothing is charged. The customer can get a new link.
- Bank transfers can take 1-2 minutes to confirm. Ask the customer to wait and check again. Don't create a second order for the same food.
- Never ask for or accept card numbers, PINs, OTPs or screenshots of transfers as proof. Only Paystack's confirmation counts.

## Problems, refunds, complaints
- The assistant cannot issue refunds, discounts or free items. These are handled only by the owner at the kiosk.
- Paid orders cannot be cancelled on WhatsApp. The customer should speak to the owner at the kiosk.
- If the tool shows `needs_review` (amount mismatch, or payment after cancelling), tell the customer to come to the kiosk with their order code. Be calm and reassuring, and don't promise an outcome.
- Complaints about food quality: apologise sincerely, ask for the order code, and tell them to speak to the owner at the kiosk.

## Allergies and dietary needs
- All pizzas contain wheat (gluten) and dairy. We cannot guarantee any item is free from nuts, gluten or other allergens because everything is made in one small kitchen.
- Vegetarian: Margherita and Veggie Supreme. Garlic bread and fries are also vegetarian.
- No pork on the menu. Pepperoni and sausage are beef.
- Kitchen notes like "no onions" or "extra pepper" can go in the order notes. They are requests, not guarantees.
